# Glossary

## Records

**Club record**
The best performance in one record category by a flight filed under the club on the BGA Ladder and launched at Gransden Lodge, following BGA Ladder rules. Records set before this rule was adopted stand as they are.
_Avoid_: club best, trophy

**Record category**
One kind of performance a record is held for, such as a task type over a set distance.

**Record holder**
The pilot whose flight holds a club record now.

**Superseded record**
A former club record, kept as part of the category's history after a better flight beat it.

**Candidate record**
A ladder flight that appears to beat a club record but has not yet been approved. It becomes a club record only once approved.
_Avoid_: new record, suspected record

**UK record**
A BGA record for a flight flown in the UK, held per sailplane class (Open, 20m, 15m, Standard) with separate feminine records. A club record may also be a UK record; whether it still is has to be checked against the BGA's list.
_Avoid_: national record (the BGA uses that for British pilots flying anywhere in the world)
